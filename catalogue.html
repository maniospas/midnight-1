Capture utopia points

Howdy expedition commander! Mission briefer here. You are to capture utopia structures like oil wells or warehouses. Alongside other structures or mechanical units ("mechas" per bunker slang) that let our military control utopia sources. When world pollution fills, return safely and you will be set for life in the bunker hierarchy.

Do not forget; the most utopic our bunker, the more people will stay with us instead of leaving in desperation and die out in the fumes. We must walk away with more utopia than any other enemy.

Prevent enemies from gaining control of our structures; utopia only matters at the end, after all. Be especially vigilant against late rushes. Be the one to do it instead. Depending on circumstance, there are other ways to increase our utopia too; you may encounter espers that have stayed aboveground, or techs like PROPAGANDA. By the way, there is a compedium of all capitalized techs at the end.

Heroes and veterans

Units earn experience from kills and captures. With enough experience they become veterans, and afterwards heroes! Each promotion increases size, damage, and max health. Heroes keep improving with random stat bonuses, including to sight and speed. Humans progress like so:

Human (same for other units)
1 DMG, 5 HP
→
Veteran
2 DMG, 10 HP
→
Hero
3 DMG, 20 HP, random upgrades

Mechas can gain experience too! However, they need the INDUSTRY tech for this. HEROICS tech gives veterans and heroes 30% dodge. HELLBRINGER gives them faster fire rate.

Movement, sight, and terrain

All kinds of terrain are meaningful. Units will move slower in rough terrain, and have increased or decreased sight -meaning attack range too- elsewhere. As a general rule, all non-grass terrain hampers speed but also grants dodging to units inside. It also restricts sight, unless units move higher. Leverage differences at the points where terrain changes to gain an advantage. Or straight up use advantageous. For example, dealing with animals is easier in forests but you can stall a lot with the added dodging by placing units in water.

Grass

Full speed. Neutral sight. Most structures can be found here.

Forest

Slow. Bad sight. Roombas lurk here. AGILE tech reduces penalty.

Desert

Slightly slow. Bad sight. More oil and mechas here. AGILE reduces penalty.

Hills

Slightly slow. Double sight. Rats and railguns here.

Mountain

Slow. Double sight. Mines here. Snowman habitat.

Water

Very slow. SEAFARING makes water a speed boost instead.

Snow

Appears on high ground. Snowmen move fast here.

Industry and spawning

Industry is a capacity score (from fields, mines, labs, etc.). Your army's combined HP/5 is the member count. Camps spawn humans only when members < industry. Going over cap stops spawning, cuts capture heal rate to 30%, and slightly speeds up the game timer.

A huge industry also accelerates pollution. Make sure that you put it to use in time for obtaining utopia before pollution fills back.
Techs

Research fills automatically (labs speed it up). Once full, open the tech tree with Esc and click a node to unlock it. Each tech costs one full research bar.

Bioweapon, Propaganda, Superiority, Hivemind, and AI Farm are ethically "iffy". Winning while holding any of them does not unlock starting perks.
